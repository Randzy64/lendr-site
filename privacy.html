Privacy Policy for Lendr

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Introduction

Lendr is a media collection management app developed by Nathan Rands. This privacy policy explains how we handle your data when you use the Lendr Android application.

We believe in a minimal-data approach. Lendr does not contain advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics services.

Information We Collect

To provide Lendr's core features, we process the following categories of data:

  • Account Information: When you sign up, we store your email address and a unique Firebase User ID.
  • Profile Data: Your display name and any profile picture you upload. Note that Lendr allows you to enter a manual location name on your profile; the app does not access your device's GPS or precise location.
  • Library & Catalogue: Information about the items you add to your shelf (books, movies, games, etc.), including titles, metadata, and custom covers.
  • Social Connections: Friend relationships and pending requests used to facilitate collection sharing.
  • Lending Records: History of items borrowed or lent, including waitlists and return reminders.
  • Device Tokens: We use Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) tokens to send you notifications about loans and wishlist availability.

How We Use Your Information

Your data is used solely to operate the app's features:

  • Managing your media library and wishlist.
  • Facilitating the borrowing and lending process with your friends.
  • Sending functional notifications (e.g., return reminders).
  • Moderating "Community Covers" (artwork contributed by users) using Google Cloud Vision SafeSearch to ensure a safe experience for all users.

Service Providers

We use trusted infrastructure providers to host and secure your data. These providers do not "buy" your data; they process it as part of providing their services to Lendr:

  • Google Firebase: Used for account authentication, database hosting (Firestore), and push notifications (FCM).
  • Supabase: Used for secure storage of profile avatars and custom item covers.
  • Cloudflare: We use a Cloudflare Worker as a secure proxy to fetch game metadata and artwork from external providers.
  • Third-Party Metadata: When you search for items or scan barcodes, titles and artwork are retrieved from external catalogues.

Camera Access

Lendr requests permission to use your camera solely for the purpose of scanning barcodes to identify items and for taking profile or cover photos. Camera data is processed on-device for scanning and is only uploaded if you choose to save a photo as a profile picture or item cover.

Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account at any time through the in-app settings or by contacting us.

  • Account Deletion: Deleting your account removes your authentication record, your personal profile, your library items, and your lending history.
  • Community Covers: Community Cover contributions may be retained after account deletion, but they are unshared and permanently disassociated from your account. The uploader is replaced with "deleted_user".

Security

We implement industry-standard security practices provided by our infrastructure partners (Firebase and Supabase) to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
