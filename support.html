Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a book?

You can add items by tapping the '+' icon on the Shelf screen. You can scan a barcode using your camera or search by title.

Can my friends see my whole library?

Only people you have accepted as friends in the app can see your library items. You can see who your friends are in the Friends tab.

Does Lendr cost anything?

Lendr is currently a passion project and is provided as-is without any subscription fees or in-app purchases.
