Delete Your Lendr Account

We respect your right to control your data. If you no longer wish to use Lendr, you can permanently delete your account and associated personal information.

Developer: Nathan Rands

Warning: Deletion is Permanent

Deleting your account will immediately and permanently remove your library, wishlist, and lending history. This action cannot be undone.

Method 1: In the App (Recommended)

The fastest way to delete your account is directly within the Lendr Android application:

  1. Open the Lendr app.
  2. Tap the Settings icon in the bottom navigation bar (the gear icon).
  3. Scroll down to the ACCOUNT section.
  4. Tap Delete Account.
  5. Confirm the deletion in the dialogue box.

Method 2: Request Deletion via Email

If you no longer have the app installed or cannot access your account, you can request manual deletion by contacting our support team.

Please send an email from the address associated with your Lendr account to ensure we can verify your identity.

Request Account Deletion

Note: For your security, we may require additional verification before processing a manual deletion request.

What data is deleted?

When you delete your account, Lendr removes the following:

  • Your login credentials and authentication record.
  • Your personal profile (name, avatar, and location name).
  • Your entire media library and wishlist.
  • Your records of items currently lent to or borrowed from friends.
  • Any pending friend requests or notifications.
  • Your device registration tokens used for push notifications.

What data remains?

To preserve the integrity of the shared library, some non-identifying data may remain:

  • Community Covers: Community Cover contributions may be retained after account deletion, but they are unshared and permanently disassociated from your account. The uploader is replaced with "deleted_user".
  • Historical Notifications: Notifications sent to other users (e.g., a message that you lent them a book) will remain in their history but will no longer link to your profile.
